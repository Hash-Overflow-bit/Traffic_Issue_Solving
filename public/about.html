About Traffic QR

Traffic QR is a fast, client-side web tool that helps you create secure QR codes containing driver and vehicle information. Use it to store or share driver name, license number, car registration and contact details in a compact, scannable format.

Why Traffic QR?

We focus on privacy and simplicity. All QR data is generated in your browser and never sent to a server. That means quick generation, no account needed, and better control over your data.

Key features

  • Generate QR codes for driver info (name, license, car number)
  • Download QR as PNG for printing or sharing
  • Data stays on your device — no server uploads
  • Simple, mobile-friendly interface
