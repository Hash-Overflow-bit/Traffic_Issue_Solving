Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate a QR?

Fill the form on the homepage with the driver name, ID/license number, phone and car number. Click "Generate QR" and the QR will appear in the panel. You can then download it as PNG.

Is my data stored on the server?

No. All data is generated and stored locally in your browser's localStorage (if you choose to save). We do not send user-entered data to any server.

What fields are supported?

Currently: name, ID card/license, phone number, car number. We may expand fields in future versions.

Can I edit the QR data later?

Yes — you can change the form values and regenerate or download a new QR.
